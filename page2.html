Products and services

Tools for safer, more informed flights

Our business offers a few different parts of a flight monitoring system. Each service focuses on getting important flight information to the people who need it.

Our service catalog

📡

SkyLink Satellite Updates

Transmits selected position, altitude, and timing updates to our monitoring hub while the aircraft is in flight.

$1,200 / aircraft / month

⚠️

FlightWatch Alert System

Checks incoming updates for missing transmissions or unusual altitude readings and helps analysts spot possible issues.

$350 / aircraft / month

🗂️

Flight Record Data Hub

Organizes flight readings and links them to aircraft, maintenance records, and investigation reports.

$850 / team / month

🛠️

Recovery Review Package

Gives approved staff a last known position and organized flight history to support a post-incident review.

$2,400 / incident review

How it works

1

Connect

Aircraft equipment sends selected readings by satellite.

2

Monitor

The system stores updates and checks for gaps or unusual values.

3

Review

Authorized staff review flight data, alerts, and related records.
