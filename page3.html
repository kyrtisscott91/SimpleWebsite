About our team

We started with a question about black boxes.

We looked at what happens when flight data is hard to access after an aircraft incident. We wanted to come up with a way for important updates to be sent through satellite communication and saved in one shared system.

We put together a fictional Honeywell Aerospace monitoring concept that connects flight data analysts, maintenance engineers, and investigators.

Frequently asked questions

Is this the real Honeywell Aerospace website?

No. This is a fictional business site made by three students for a class assignment.

Can we actually buy these services?

No, the services and prices are examples to show our business idea.

What makes our idea different?

It focuses on getting selected flight updates to a shared database during the flight instead of relying only on retrieving a physical recorder later.
